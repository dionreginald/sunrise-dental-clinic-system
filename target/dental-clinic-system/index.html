Help

    
    
    
      
  1. Log in with your staff username and password.
    2. 
      
  2. Use "Register Appointment" to add a new patient visit.
    3. 
      
  3. Use "Display Appointment" to look up an existing appointment by its number.
    4. 
      
  4. Use "Cancel Appointment" to cancel a booking.
    5. 
      
  5. Use "Calculate & Print Bill" once treatment is complete to preview a bill, then click "Save Bill" to record it (and "Print Bill" to print it).
    6. 
      
  6. Use "Display Bills" to see every bill that has been saved.
    7. 
      
  7. Use "Manage Dentists & Treatments" to add new dentists or treatment types — they'll immediately show up in the Register Appointment dropdowns.
