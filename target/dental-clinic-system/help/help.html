Staff Help Guide

  1. Login — enter your staff username and password on the login screen.
  2. Register Appointment — open the "New Appointment" tab, fill in patient details, dentist, treatment type, date and time, then click Save. An appointment number is generated automatically.
  3. Search Appointment — open the "Search" tab and enter the appointment number to view full patient and appointment details.
  4. Billing — from a found appointment, click "Generate Bill" to preview the total cost (treatment cost + consultation fee). This preview is not saved yet. Click "Save Bill" to record it, then "Print Bill" to print the receipt.
  5. Display Bills — open the "Display Bills" tab to see every bill that has been saved.
  6. Logout — click Logout in the top right to safely end your session.
